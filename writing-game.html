How to finish the draft

  1. Survive: Your pen automatically targets the nearest typo or doubt. Collect the glowing ink drops for XP.
  2. Build: Level up to choose one of three upgrades. Stack pen damage, extra shots, orbiting commas, and revision waves.
  3. Proofread: Between chapters, attacks stop and bullet patterns take over. White bullets always hurt. Blue bullets hurt only while you move. Orange bullets hurt only when you stand still. Letter markers identify the colors too.
  4. Dash: Move and press Space or DASH for a brief invulnerable burst. The cooldown is three seconds. In gravity mode, Space / Up / W / JUMP instead makes you jump; hold for a higher jump.
  5. Final draft: Defeat the Inner Editor before the final deadline. The boss locks you in a correction box: weave through moving margins, leave warning lines before deletion beams fire, then jump over underlines when gravity turns on. Blue walls require you to stand still; orange walls require movement. Below half HP, the sequence and obstacles accelerate.

On touch screens, hold and drag the movement stick. The game pauses when you switch tabs or leave the window. No sound or device vibration is used.
